# OJAMS — User Guide & Onboarding Tutorial

## For Job Seekers (Applicants)

---

### Step 1 — Create Your Account

1. Visit the OJAMS homepage and click **Register**.
2. Fill in your **Full Name**, **Email Address**, **Contact Number**, and a **Password**.
   - Password must be at least 8 characters and contain an uppercase letter, a number, and a special character.
3. Answer the math security question (CAPTCHA) to prove you're human.
4. Click **Create Account**. You'll be redirected to the login page automatically.

---

### Step 2 — Log In

1. Go to the **Login** page.
2. Enter your registered email and password.
3. After 5 failed attempts your IP is locked out for 30 seconds.
4. If you forgot your password, click **Forgot Password** and follow the reset link sent to your email.

---

### Step 3 — Browse Jobs

1. After logging in you land on **Browse Jobs**.
2. Use the **search bar** to filter by job title or company.
3. Use the **Location** and **Job Type** dropdowns for further filtering.
4. Each card shows the applicant count, job type, deadline (if set), and status badge (Open / Closed).
5. Click **View Details** to read the full job description and qualifications.
6. Click the **bookmark icon** on any card to save the job to your **Saved Jobs** list for later.

---

### Step 4 — Apply for a Job

1. On a job card or the Job Detail page, click **Apply** / **Apply Now**.
2. The application form pre-fills your profile information (name, contact, address, birthdate). Review and update as needed.
3. Fill in your educational background (Elementary through College) and skills/work experience.
4. Optionally upload your **résumé** (PDF, DOC, or DOCX — max 5 MB).
5. Click **Submit Application**. A confirmation toast will appear.
6. You can only apply once per job posting.

---

### Step 5 — Track Your Applications

1. Click **My Applications** in the top navigation.
2. Each row shows the job title, company, date applied, and current **Status**:
   - **Pending** — under review by the admin.
   - **Approved** — your application was accepted.
   - **Rejected** — your application was not selected.
3. Click **View** to see the full details of any application.
4. For Pending applications, click **Cancel** if you wish to withdraw.

---

### Step 6 — Manage Your Profile

1. Click your name in the top-right corner and select **Profile Settings**.
2. Click **Edit Profile** to update your name, email, contact number, address, and birthdate.
3. To change your password, fill in the **Current Password** and **New Password** fields. The strength meter shows how strong your new password is.
4. To upload a profile photo, click **Change Photo** on your profile card and select an image (JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP — max 2 MB).
5. Click **Save Changes** when done.

---

### Saved Jobs

- Click the **bookmark icon** on any job card to save it.
- Access all saved jobs from the **Saved Jobs** link in the navigation bar.
- Click the **bookmark-x** button on a saved card to remove it.

---

## For Administrators

---

### Dashboard

The Dashboard shows a summary of key metrics (total jobs, total applications, pending applications, registered users) and a live **Activity Log** of recent system events. Use the search, status filter, and date range filters to find specific log entries.

---

### Manage Jobs

1. Go to **Manage Jobs** in the sidebar.
2. Click **+ Add Job** to create a new posting (title, company, description, qualifications, location, job type, salary range, deadline).
3. Click the **Edit (pencil)** icon on any row to update a job.
4. Click **Delete (trash)** to remove a job (this also deletes all its applications).
5. Use the **Search** bar and **Status / Job Type / Date** filters to find specific postings.
6. Use **Bulk Actions** (select checkboxes → choose action) to close or delete multiple jobs at once.

---

### Manage Applications

1. Go to **Applications** in the sidebar.
2. Use the search bar to filter by applicant name, email, or job title.
3. Click the **eye icon** to view full application details, educational background, and status history.
4. Click **Approve** or **Reject** in the detail modal or inline on the row.
5. Use **Bulk Approve / Bulk Reject** to process multiple applications at once.
6. The status history panel shows every status transition with a timestamp and the admin who made the change.

---

### User Management

1. Go to **User Management** in the sidebar.
2. Filter users by role (All / Admin / User) or search by name/email.
3. Click **Deactivate** to prevent a user from logging in (their data is preserved).
4. Click **Reactivate** to restore access.
5. Use **Change Role** to promote a user to Admin or demote an Admin to User.

---

### Reports

1. Go to **Reports** in the sidebar.
2. View charts showing applicants per job and monthly application trends.
3. Switch between **Chart** and **Table** views using the toggle buttons.
4. Export data using **Export CSV** (spreadsheet) or **Export PDF** (printable report) buttons.

---

### Activity Log Management

From the Dashboard, click **Clear Old Logs** to delete log entries older than a specified number of days (default: 90 days). This helps keep the log table manageable.

---

### Profile Settings (Admin)

Same as the applicant profile settings — update personal information, change password, and upload a profile photo from the top-right avatar menu.
